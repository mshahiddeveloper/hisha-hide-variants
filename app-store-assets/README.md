# HiSha App Store Listing

This folder contains the approved working copy and media plan for the public
Shopify App Store listing. Keep pricing only in the Partner Dashboard pricing
section.

## Listing identity

- App name: `HiSha: Hide Out of Stock`
- App handle: `hisha-hide-variants` (confirm availability before publishing)
- App card subtitle: `Show shoppers only products and variants they can buy`
- App introduction: `Hide sold-out choices from your storefront so shoppers focus on products and variants they can buy.`
- Merchant requirement: Online Store required
- Supported merchant UI language: English

## App details

HiSha keeps unavailable choices out of your Online Store without changing
product status in Shopify Admin. Automatically hide sold-out variants, move
shoppers to an available combination, and optionally hide fully sold-out
products from browsing. You can also manually hide selected products or
variants when connected inventory data is inaccurate. Setup uses a theme app
embed with no theme code edits. Designed for compatible Online Store themes.

## Feature bullets

- Hide sold-out variants from product page option selectors.
- Auto-select an available variant when a chosen combination is sold out.
- Optionally hide fully sold-out products from storefront browsing.
- Manually hide selected products or variants regardless of inventory.
- Keep products active and unchanged in Shopify Admin.
- Enable with a theme app embed without editing theme code.

## Submission checklist

- Upload the icon in the Shopify Dev Dashboard.
- Configure the public monthly plan and three-day trial in App Pricing.
- Add privacy policy, terms, support, developer website, and emergency contact.
- Provide a direct demo-store product URL.
- Select `Merchant must have Online Store`.
- Run automated listing and app checks.
- Verify Lighthouse performance impact stays below Shopify's review threshold.
- Submit the English listing and private review screencast.

## Claims to avoid

- Compatibility with every custom theme
- Drafting, unpublishing, or modifying Shopify products
- Inventory syncing, analytics, tags, thresholds, or regional rules
- Guaranteed conversion, ranking, or terms such as best, first, and only
